ROLE/PURPOSE: production sprite animation for the BASE BODY of an isometric paper-doll character in a 2D game (equipment is added later as separate layers).
SUBJECT: 6-frame WALK cycle toward the south-east, in place, empty hands, calm steady walk, arms swinging opposite to the legs. Frame 1: contact, near leg forward with the heel down, far leg back, near arm swung back. Frame 2: down, weight on the front leg, body lowest. Frame 3: passing, the back leg passes under the body, body highest. Frame 4: contact with the other leg forward, near arm swung forward. Frame 5: down. Frame 6: passing. Loops back to frame 1.
Attached image 1 is the approved isometric base body. Copy it exactly in every frame: the same bald head and simple face, the same plain off-white linen long-sleeve undershirt and linen trousers, the same dark brown shoes, bare skin hands, the same sturdy game proportions (head about one fifth of body height), the same 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1f33) and the same coarse pixel-block size. Crisp 16-bit pixel art, flat cel shading, light from upper left. The same isometric camera as image 1 (looking down at about 30 degrees) and the same facing in every frame: SOUTH-EAST, turned toward the viewer and to the right, face in three-quarter view, the nearer arm on the left side of the figure. No hair, no armor, no belt.
LAYOUT: one single horizontal row of frames, evenly spaced, left to right in animation order. Each frame sits in its own equal-width invisible cell with clear empty space between neighbouring frames so no two poses touch or overlap. Every frame shows the whole figure at exactly the same scale (about 50% of the image height), feet on the same horizontal line. The figure animates in place, no travel between frames.
EXCLUDE: text, frame numbers, labels, arrows, grid lines, cell borders, ground, floor tiles, cast shadows, scenery, checkerboard pattern, extra characters, motion blur, speed lines, trails, effects.
