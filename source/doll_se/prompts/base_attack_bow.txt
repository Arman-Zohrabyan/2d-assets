ROLE/PURPOSE: production sprite animation for the BASE BODY of an isometric paper-doll character in a 2D game, with its weapon (armour is added later as separate layers).
SUBJECT: 6-frame ARCHERY animation for a game (sport archery practice toward a straw target far off-screen to the south-east (toward the viewer and to the right)), with a simple wooden longbow (#6b4423, #8a5a2e, #a87440) with a thin light string (#e8e0c8) and one plain wooden practice arrow with white feathers. The bow is held in the LEFT hand (the far hand, on the right side of the figure) with that arm stretched forward toward the target; the RIGHT hand pulls the string. Frame 1: relaxed, bow held low in front. Frame 2: raising the bow to shoulder height and setting the practice arrow on the string. Frame 3: string pulled halfway. Frame 4: string fully pulled back to the cheek, the arrow pointing toward the target. Frame 5: the arrow has just been let go and is gone, the string is straight again, the right hand opens. Frame 6: lowering the bow back to relaxed.
Attached image 1 is the approved base body for this view and image 2 its approved idle animation. Copy the body exactly in every frame: the same bald head, the same plain off-white linen long-sleeve undershirt and linen trousers, the same dark brown shoes, bare skin hands, the same sturdy game proportions (head about one fifth of body height), the same 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1f33) and the same coarse pixel-block size. Crisp 16-bit pixel art, flat cel shading, light from upper left. The same isometric camera (looking down at about 30 degrees) and the same facing in every frame: SOUTH-EAST: turned toward the viewer and to the right, face in three-quarter view, the nearer arm on the left side of the figure. No hair, no armor, no belt.
LAYOUT: a grid of 2 rows x 3 columns = 6 frames, read left to right, top row first. Each frame sits in its own equal invisible cell with wide empty space around it, so no pose or weapon touches a neighbouring frame or the image edge. Every frame shows the whole figure at exactly the same scale (about 30% of the image height), feet on the same ground line within each row. The figure stays in place inside its cell.
EXCLUDE: text, frame numbers, labels, arrows drawn as UI, grid lines, cell borders, ground, floor tiles, cast shadows, scenery, checkerboard pattern, extra characters, motion blur, speed lines, trails, effects, shields, armour, hair.
