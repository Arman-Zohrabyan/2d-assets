ROLE/PURPOSE: production sprite animation for the BASE BODY of an isometric paper-doll character in a 2D game (with its weapon; armour is added later as separate layers).
SUBJECT: two short animations in one sheet, one per row. TOP ROW, frames 1-3: HIT REACTION: the character recoils as if bumped hard from the front; empty hands, no weapon. Frame 1: struck: the head jerks back, the upper body bends back, both arms jerk a little out from the sides, knees bent. Frame 2: staggering half a step back, hunched forward, winded, arms low at the sides. Frame 3: recovering, almost back to the relaxed stance of image 2. BOTTOM ROW, frames 4-6: GUARD WITH THE SHIELD ARM, holding a classic fantasy one-handed sword: straight steel blade about as long as the figure's leg (#8b9bb4, highlight #c0cbdc, dark navy outline), small gold crossguard and round gold pommel (#c99a2e, #f2d16b), short dark brown grip, in the RIGHT hand (the near hand, on the left side of the figure). Frame 4: starting the guard: the LEFT forearm (on the right side of the figure) coming up, the sword held low and forward. Frame 5: guard: the LEFT forearm (on the right side of the figure) raised level in front of the chest at shoulder height, the fist clenched, as if a shield were strapped to it in front of the chest and chin; the sword held back at hip height, pointing forward, ready; knees bent, braced. Frame 6: the same guard held firmly, braced a little lower. Do NOT draw any shield: the left fist is empty (the shield is added later as a separate layer).
Attached image 1 is the approved base body for this view and image 2 its approved idle animation. Copy the body exactly in every frame: the same bald head, the same plain off-white linen long-sleeve undershirt and linen trousers, the same dark brown shoes, bare skin hands, the same sturdy game proportions (head about one fifth of body height), the same 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1f33) and the same coarse pixel-block size. Crisp 16-bit pixel art, flat cel shading, light from upper left. The same isometric camera (looking down at about 30 degrees) and the same facing in every frame: SOUTH-EAST: turned toward the viewer and to the right, face in three-quarter view, the nearer arm on the left side of the figure. No hair, no armor, no belt.
LAYOUT: a grid of 2 rows x 3 columns = 6 frames, read left to right, top row first. Each frame sits in its own equal invisible cell with wide empty space around it, so no pose or weapon touches a neighbouring frame or the image edge. Every frame shows the whole figure at EXACTLY the same scale (about 30% of the image height when standing), the head the same size in every frame, feet on the same ground line within each row. The figure stays in place in the middle of its cell.
EXCLUDE: text, frame numbers, labels, arrows, grid lines, cell borders, ground, floor tiles, cast shadows, scenery, checkerboard pattern, extra characters, motion blur, speed lines, trails, effects, blood, armour, hair, shields.
