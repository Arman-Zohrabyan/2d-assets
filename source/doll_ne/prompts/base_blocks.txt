ROLE/PURPOSE: production sprite animation for the BASE BODY of an isometric paper-doll character in a 2D game (with its weapon; armour is added later as separate layers).
SUBJECT: two short PARRY animations in one sheet, one per row: the weapon is raised to block an incoming blow. TOP ROW, frames 1-3: PARRY WITH A ONE-HANDED SWORD: a classic fantasy one-handed sword: straight steel blade about as long as the figure's leg (#8b9bb4, highlight #c0cbdc, dark navy outline), small gold crossguard and round gold pommel (#c99a2e, #f2d16b), short dark brown grip, in the RIGHT hand (the near hand, on the right side of the figure); the left hand stays empty. Frame 1: ready stance, knees slightly bent, the sword held forward at waist height. Frame 2: lifting the sword up in front of the body. Frame 3: parry: the sword raised in front of the body, the hilt at shoulder height and the blade standing up in front of the face, slanting a little, to catch a blow; knees bent, braced; the empty left arm held back for balance. BOTTOM ROW, frames 4-6: PARRY WITH A TWO-HANDED GREATSWORD: a big two-handed greatsword: very long straight steel blade almost as long as the figure is tall (#8b9bb4, highlight #c0cbdc, dark navy outline), wide steel crossguard, long dark brown leather grip, round steel pommel, with BOTH hands together on the long grip, the right hand above the left. Frame 4: guard stance, the greatsword held upright in front of the body, tip up, hands at the belly. Frame 5: lifting the greatsword up. Frame 6: high parry: the greatsword raised and held above the head, nearly level across the picture, both hands together on the grip at one end of it; knees bent, braced.
Attached image 1 is the approved base body for this view and image 2 its approved idle animation. Copy the body exactly in every frame: the same bald head, the same plain off-white linen long-sleeve undershirt and linen trousers, the same dark brown shoes, bare skin hands, the same sturdy game proportions (head about one fifth of body height), the same 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1f33) and the same coarse pixel-block size. Crisp 16-bit pixel art, flat cel shading, light from upper left. The same isometric camera (looking down at about 30 degrees) and the same facing in every frame: NORTH-EAST: away from the viewer and to the right, a three-quarter BACK VIEW, the back of the bald head and the right ear, no eyes, the nearer (right) arm on the right side of the figure. No hair, no armor, no belt.
LAYOUT: a grid of 2 rows x 3 columns = 6 frames, read left to right, top row first. Each frame sits in its own equal invisible cell with wide empty space around it, so no pose or weapon touches a neighbouring frame or the image edge. Every frame shows the whole figure at EXACTLY the same scale (about 30% of the image height when standing), the head the same size in every frame, feet on the same ground line within each row. The figure stays in place in the middle of its cell.
EXCLUDE: text, frame numbers, labels, arrows, grid lines, cell borders, ground, floor tiles, cast shadows, scenery, checkerboard pattern, extra characters, motion blur, speed lines, trails, effects, blood, armour, hair, shields.
