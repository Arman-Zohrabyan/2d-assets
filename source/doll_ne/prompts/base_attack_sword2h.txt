ROLE/PURPOSE: production sprite animation for the BASE BODY of an isometric paper-doll character in a 2D game, with its weapon (armour is added later as separate layers).
SUBJECT: 6-frame heavy two-handed GREATSWORD SWING toward to the north-east (away from the viewer and to the right) (a practice swing for a game move), holding a big two-handed greatsword: very long straight steel blade almost as long as the figure is tall (#8b9bb4, highlight #c0cbdc, dark navy outline), wide steel crossguard, long dark brown leather grip, round steel pommel, with BOTH hands on the grip, the right hand above the left. The hands and arms NEVER go above the top of the head and never cover the face. Frame 1: guard stance, greatsword held diagonally in front of the body, tip up and forward. Frame 2: wind-up, greatsword pulled back over the right shoulder, both hands at shoulder height in front of the chest, blade pointing backward and slightly up behind the back. Frame 3: powerful swing forward, blade horizontal in front of the body at chest height, arms extended forward. Frame 4: follow-through, blade low in front with the tip near the ground, knees bent, body leaning forward. Frame 5: lifting the greatsword back up in front of the body. Frame 6: guard stance like frame 1.
Attached image 1 is the approved base body for this view and image 2 its approved idle animation. Copy the body exactly in every frame: the same bald head, the same plain off-white linen long-sleeve undershirt and linen trousers, the same dark brown shoes, bare skin hands, the same sturdy game proportions (head about one fifth of body height), the same 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1f33) and the same coarse pixel-block size. Crisp 16-bit pixel art, flat cel shading, light from upper left. The same isometric camera (looking down at about 30 degrees) and the same facing in every frame: NORTH-EAST: away from the viewer and to the right, a three-quarter BACK VIEW, the back of the bald head and the right ear, no eyes, the nearer (right) arm on the right side of the figure. No hair, no armor, no belt.
LAYOUT: a grid of 2 rows x 3 columns = 6 frames, read left to right, top row first. Each frame sits in its own equal invisible cell with wide empty space around it, so no pose or weapon touches a neighbouring frame or the image edge. Every frame shows the whole figure at exactly the same scale (about 30% of the image height), feet on the same ground line within each row. The figure stays in place inside its cell.
EXCLUDE: text, frame numbers, labels, arrows drawn as UI, grid lines, cell borders, ground, floor tiles, cast shadows, scenery, checkerboard pattern, extra characters, motion blur, speed lines, trails, effects, shields, armour, hair.
