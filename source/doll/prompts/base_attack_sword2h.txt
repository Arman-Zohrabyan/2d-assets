ROLE/PURPOSE: production sprite animation for the BASE BODY of a paper-doll character in a 2D side-scrolling action game (equipment is added later as separate layers).
SUBJECT: 6-frame heavy two-handed GREATSWORD SWING (a practice swing for a game move), holding a big two-handed greatsword: very long straight steel blade almost as long as the figure is tall (#8b9bb4, highlight #c0cbdc, dark navy outline), wide steel crossguard, long dark brown leather grip, round steel pommel, with BOTH hands on the grip. The hands and arms NEVER go above the top of the head and never cover the face. Frame 1: guard stance, greatsword held diagonally in front of the body, tip up and forward. Frame 2: wind-up, greatsword pulled back over the NEAR shoulder, both hands at shoulder height in front of the chest, blade pointing backward and slightly up behind the back. Frame 3: powerful swing forward, blade horizontal in front of the body at chest height, arms extended forward. Frame 4: follow-through, blade low in front with the tip near the ground, knees bent, body leaning forward. Frame 5: lifting the greatsword back up in front of the body. Frame 6: guard stance like frame 1.
Attached image 1 is the approved base body. Copy it exactly in every frame: the same bald head and simple face, the same plain off-white linen long-sleeve undershirt and linen trousers, the same dark brown shoes, bare skin hands, the same sturdy game proportions (head about one fifth of body height), the same 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1f33) and the same coarse pixel-block size. Crisp 16-bit pixel art, flat cel shading, light from upper left. Pure side profile facing RIGHT in every frame. No hair, no armor, no belt.
LAYOUT: a grid of 2 rows x 3 columns = 6 frames, read left to right, top row first. Each frame sits in its own equal invisible cell with wide empty space around it, so no pose or weapon touches a neighbouring frame or the image edge. Every frame shows the whole figure at exactly the same scale (about 30% of the image height), feet on the same ground line within each row. The figure stays in place inside its cell.
EXCLUDE: text, frame numbers, labels, arrows, grid lines, cell borders, ground, cast shadows, scenery, checkerboard pattern, extra characters, motion blur, speed lines, trails, effects.
