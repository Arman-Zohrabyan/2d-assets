ROLE/PURPOSE: production sprite animation for the BASE BODY of an isometric paper-doll character in a 2D game (armour is added later as separate layers).
SUBJECT: 6-frame DEFEAT animation for a game (knocked out, the character collapses to the ground); empty hands, no weapon. Frame 1: staggered: the head thrown back, the chest pushed back, arms flung out from the sides. Frame 2: knees buckling, the body sagging, the head drooping. Frame 3: dropping onto the knees, the upper body slumping. Frame 4: toppling sideways to the left of the image, the body falling at about 45 degrees. Frame 5: hitting the ground. Frame 6: lying still. The character falls over SIDEWAYS toward the left of the image, so it ends lying on the ground stretched out level across the picture, the head on the LEFT and the feet on the RIGHT, the body seen from behind; in frames 5 and 6 the whole body lies flat on the ground line, arms limp, the hands open on the ground beside the body. The body keeps exactly the same size when lying as when standing (the head the same size in every frame).
Attached image 1 is the approved base body for this view and image 2 its approved idle animation. Copy the body exactly in every frame: the same bald head, the same plain off-white linen long-sleeve undershirt and linen trousers, the same dark brown shoes, bare skin hands, the same sturdy game proportions (head about one fifth of body height), the same 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1f33) and the same coarse pixel-block size. Crisp 16-bit pixel art, flat cel shading, light from upper left. The same isometric camera (looking down at about 30 degrees) and the same facing in every frame: NORTH: straight AWAY from the viewer, a BACK VIEW, the back of the bald head, no face visible. No hair, no armor, no belt.
LAYOUT: a grid of 2 rows x 3 columns = 6 frames, read left to right, top row first. Each frame sits in its own equal invisible cell with wide empty space around it, so no pose or weapon touches a neighbouring frame or the image edge. Every frame shows the whole figure at EXACTLY the same scale (about 30% of the image height when standing), the head the same size in every frame, feet on the same ground line within each row. The figure stays in place in the middle of its cell.
EXCLUDE: text, frame numbers, labels, arrows, grid lines, cell borders, ground, floor tiles, cast shadows, scenery, checkerboard pattern, extra characters, motion blur, speed lines, trails, effects, blood, armour, hair, weapons, shields.
