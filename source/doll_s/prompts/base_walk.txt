ROLE/PURPOSE: production sprite animation for the BASE BODY of an isometric paper-doll character in a 2D game (equipment is added later as separate layers).
SUBJECT: 6-frame WALK cycle toward the viewer (south), in place, empty hands, calm steady walk, arms swinging opposite to the legs. Frame 1: contact, the leg on the left side of the image forward (its foot a little lower in the image) with the heel down, the other leg back, the arm on the right side of the image swung forward. Frame 2: down, weight on the front leg, body lowest. Frame 3: passing, the legs pass each other under the body, body highest. Frame 4: contact with the other leg forward (the leg on the right side of the image), the arm on the left side swung forward. Frame 5: down. Frame 6: passing. Loops back to frame 1. The body stays symmetrical and frontal in every frame.
Attached image 1 is the approved base body for this view. Copy it exactly in every frame: the same bald head, the same plain off-white linen long-sleeve undershirt and linen trousers, the same dark brown shoes, bare skin hands, the same sturdy game proportions (head about one fifth of body height), the same 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1f33) and the same coarse pixel-block size. Image 2 is the same mannequin from the south-east, for its build and proportions. Crisp 16-bit pixel art, flat cel shading, light from upper left. The same isometric camera as image 1 (looking down at about 30 degrees) and the same facing in every frame: SOUTH, straight toward the viewer: a symmetrical FRONT VIEW, the face looking at the viewer with both eyes, both arms and both legs fully visible. No hair, no armor, no belt.
LAYOUT: one single horizontal row of frames, evenly spaced, left to right in animation order. Each frame sits in its own equal-width invisible cell with clear empty space between neighbouring frames so no two poses touch or overlap. Every frame shows the whole figure at exactly the same scale (about 50% of the image height), feet on the same horizontal line. The figure animates in place, no travel between frames.
EXCLUDE: text, frame numbers, labels, arrows, grid lines, cell borders, ground, floor tiles, cast shadows, scenery, checkerboard pattern, extra characters, motion blur, speed lines, trails, effects.
