ROLE/PURPOSE: production sprite animation for the BASE BODY of an isometric paper-doll character in a 2D game, with its weapon (armour is added later as separate layers).
SUBJECT: 6-frame heavy two-handed OVERHEAD GREATSWORD CHOP toward the viewer (a practice move for a game): a straight vertical chop from high above the head down in front of the body, so the sword moves UP and DOWN in the picture, never out to the side. A big two-handed greatsword: very long straight steel blade almost as long as the figure is tall (#8b9bb4, highlight #c0cbdc, dark navy outline), wide steel crossguard, long dark brown leather grip, round steel pommel, with BOTH hands on the grip, the right hand above the left. Frame 1: guard stance, the greatsword held upright in front of the body, tip up, hands at the belly. Frame 2: wind-up, the greatsword raised up beside the head over the RIGHT shoulder (on the left side of the image), both hands next to the right ear, NOT over the head and NOT in front of the face, the blade pointing up and back; the whole face stays visible. Frame 3: chopping down, the blade coming down in front of the body on the centre line, hands at chest height. Frame 4: follow-through, the blade low in front of the body pointing down and forward, hands at the hips, knees a little bent, the figure still standing. Frame 5: lifting the greatsword back up in front of the body. Frame 6: guard stance like frame 1.
Attached image 1 is the approved base body for this view and image 2 its approved idle animation. Copy the body exactly in every frame: the same bald head, the same plain off-white linen long-sleeve undershirt and linen trousers, the same dark brown shoes, bare skin hands, the same sturdy game proportions (head about one fifth of body height), the same 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1f33) and the same coarse pixel-block size. Crisp 16-bit pixel art, flat cel shading, light from upper left. The same isometric camera (looking down at about 30 degrees) and the same facing in every frame: SOUTH: straight toward the viewer, a FRONT VIEW, the face looking at the viewer with both eyes. No hair, no armor, no belt.
LAYOUT: a grid of 2 rows x 3 columns = 6 frames, read left to right, top row first. Each frame sits in its own equal invisible cell with wide empty space around it, so no pose or weapon touches a neighbouring frame or the image edge. Every frame shows the whole figure at EXACTLY the same scale (about 30% of the image height), the head the same size in every frame, feet on the same ground line within each row. The figure stays in place inside its cell.
EXCLUDE: text, frame numbers, labels, grid lines, cell borders, ground, floor tiles, cast shadows, scenery, checkerboard pattern, extra characters, motion blur, speed lines, trails, effects, shields, armour, hair, sideways swings.
