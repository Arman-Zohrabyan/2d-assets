ROLE/PURPOSE: production sprite animation for the BASE BODY of an isometric paper-doll character in a 2D game (equipment is added later as separate layers).
SUBJECT: 6-frame RUN cycle toward the viewer (south); the leg and arm swinging forward come toward the viewer and appear a little lower in the image, in place, empty hands, body leaning forward about 15 degrees, long strides, arms bent at the elbows and pumping opposite to the legs. Frame 1: contact, one leg reaching forward. Frame 2: down, front knee bent. Frame 3: flight, both feet off the ground. Frame 4: contact with the other leg forward. Frame 5: down. Frame 6: flight. Loops back to frame 1.
Attached image 1 is the approved base body for this view and image 2 its approved walk animation. Copy the body exactly in every frame: the same bald head, the same plain off-white linen long-sleeve undershirt and linen trousers, the same dark brown shoes, bare skin hands, the same sturdy game proportions (head about one fifth of body height), the same 1-pixel dark navy outline (#1b1f33) and the same coarse pixel-block size, the same scale as image 2. Crisp 16-bit pixel art, flat cel shading, light from upper left. The same isometric camera (looking down at about 30 degrees) and the same facing in every frame: SOUTH: straight toward the viewer, a symmetrical FRONT VIEW, the face looking at the viewer with both eyes. No hair, no armor, no belt.
LAYOUT: one single horizontal row of frames, evenly spaced, left to right in animation order. Each frame sits in its own equal-width invisible cell with clear empty space between neighbouring frames so no two poses touch or overlap. Every frame shows the whole figure at exactly the same scale (about 50% of the image height), feet on the same horizontal line (feet in flight a little above it). The figure animates in place, no travel between frames.
EXCLUDE: text, frame numbers, labels, arrows, grid lines, cell borders, ground, floor tiles, cast shadows, scenery, checkerboard pattern, extra characters, motion blur, speed lines, trails, effects.
